# Strategy portfolio

V1 contains three structural research tracks: binary complete sets, validated
vanilla NegRisk portfolios, and a Holding Rewards complete-set pilot. V2 adds only
shadow market-making research unless separately promoted. Every strategy is a pure
proposal generator and remains unproven until replay, paper, shadow, accounting, and
promotion gates pass. No strategy may call execution or authorize its own risk.

Cross-event combinatorial solving and LLM-assisted candidate relationships are later
work. Martingale, manipulation, restriction bypass, and LLM-only betting are rejected
defaults. The system is expected to emit no proposal when proof or inputs are weak.
